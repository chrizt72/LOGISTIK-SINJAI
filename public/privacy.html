1. Overview

SINJAI BENDERANG ("we", "our", or "the Application") is an operational web application built for warehouse material inventory tracking (QR scanning), Yantek & HVB field work realization reporting, and distribution transformer (Trafo) mutation/insertion logging for PLN UP3 Bulukumba — ULP Sinjai.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we access, how we use and protect Google user data, and what rights users have over their data.

2. Information We Collect & Google API Scopes

When an authorized officer voluntarily signs in using Google OAuth 2.0, the Application requests only the permissions necessary to synchronize operational logs with the user's designated Google Workspace spreadsheets:

  • Basic Google Account Information (userinfo.email, userinfo.profile): Used solely to display the signed-in officer's name, email address, and profile avatar inside the Application and attribute audit logs created by that officer.
  • Google Drive & Google Sheets Access (drive, drive.file, drive.readonly, spreadsheets, spreadsheets.readonly): Used solely to list, locate, read, create, and append rows to the user's authorized operational spreadsheets (such as LOGISTIK SINJAI and TEKNIK SINJAI) for warehouse inbound/outbound material tracking, Yantek/HVB work reports, and transformer management records.
  • Operational Inputs Provided by Users: Material serial numbers scanned via camera/QR scanner, officer names, document numbers, work realization quantities, and GPS coordinates explicitly submitted by the user in operational forms.

3. How We Use Information

We use accessed data strictly to provide and operate the Application's core features:

  • Authenticating authorized personnel via Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication.
  • Reading and updating warehouse stock and technical realization sheets in Google Sheets.
  • Generating PDF official handover reports (Berita Acara) and CSV exports requested by the user.
  • Maintaining offline/local browser continuity on mobile field devices.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or use Google user data or spreadsheet contents for advertising, marketing, profiling, or artificial intelligence model training.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Limited Use Disclosure: SINJAI BENDERANG's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

  • In-Memory OAuth Tokens: Google OAuth access tokens are stored strictly in active browser session memory during use and are cleared immediately when the user signs out or closes the session.
  • Encrypted Transport: All communication between your device, the Application (https://sinjaibenderang.netlify.app/), Firebase, and Google Workspace APIs occurs over HTTPS/TLS.
  • No External Third-Party Data Brokers: Operational spreadsheet data is transmitted directly from your browser to Google Sheets APIs under your account's authorization. Local browser cache (for offline field resilience) stays on your device and can be cleared at any time.

6. User Control, Data Deletion & Revoking Access

  • You may sign out of the Application at any time using the Ganti Akun / Keluar button, which clears the active session token from memory.
  • You may revoke the Application's access to your Google account at any time via Google Account Permissions (myaccount.google.com/permissions).
  • To request deletion of any stored operational records or ask questions regarding privacy, contact our administrator at chrizt72@gmail.com.
